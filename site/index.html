Complementary By Construction

Forking is a typed function of the anchor set, not a menu pick. The selection returns one best-deepen and one best-diverge, guaranteeing different directions.

A novelty ledger terminates saturated branches so recursion cannot collapse into restatement or expensive echo chambers. Scale to as many loops as you have tokens.
